Good to know

What is Room to Say?

A project to help people prepare for healthcare conversations by bringing together their own notes, questions, and experiences.

Can I download the app?

The app is in development. Download information will be added here when it becomes available.

Does it provide medical advice?

No. Its intended purpose is to help you prepare and communicate with your healthcare team. Decisions about diagnosis, treatment, and medicines belong in conversations with qualified healthcare professionals.

Can I use the preparation prompts now?

Yes. The homepage has three simple prompts you can tick or print. The ticks are temporary and are not saved or sent by this website.

What if I have an urgent health concern?

This website is not monitored for urgent requests. Contact your healthcare team or local emergency services as appropriate.
