APPOINTMENT PREPARATION GUIDES

A little help
before you go.

Not sure what to say to your doctor? Worried you’ll forget a question, or unsure what to take to your first hospital appointment? Start with a guide that meets you where you are.

  • How to talk to your doctor and explain what matters
  • Your first doctor or hospital appointment: what to take
  • Forget what to tell your doctor? A simple way to prepare
  • How to explain your medical history to a doctor

You don’t have to get it perfect.

These guides offer practical ways to organise your thoughts. They are written from a patient perspective, with links to trusted patient resources. They do not replace medical advice or your clinic’s instructions.

Room to Say is still in development. Everything here can be used with your own notebook or phone notes.
