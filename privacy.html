PRIVACY POLICY

Your information.
Your choices.

Last updated: 10 November 2026. This policy describes Room to Say version 1.0.0, build 14, prepared for the first public release, and this website. Earlier TestFlight versions with iCloud sync are addressed separately below. Publishing this policy does not announce that V1 is available.

Who operates Room to Say

Room to Say is operated by Max Poplawski personally, based in Poland. For privacy enquiries or requests, contact privacy@roomtosay.org. For app support, contact support@roomtosay.org.

The app at a glance

Version 1.0.0 (14) stores care records on your iPhone. It has no Room to Say account, developer server, advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDK. It has no iCloud sync. The app does not send your care records to Max. Apple services, information you choose to share, support enquiries and website visits are separate, as explained below.

What you can store

  • Optional profile name, age in years and conditions. The profile does not ask for a date of birth.
  • Notes, questions, detailed entries, check-in answers, medications, appointments and appointment preparation.
  • Professional names, specialties, practices and an optional photo you select.
  • Medical letters: original files, recognised text, corrections and associated history.
  • Unfinished writing, saved drafts, settings and reminder preferences.

You choose what to enter. Profile information is not automatically added to briefs, notifications or PDFs. The app uses one profile; it does not provide separate child or family profiles.

Processing on your iPhone

Letters are read using PDF text extraction and Apple Vision text recognition on the device. AI generation is disabled in this release. Manual briefs, notes documents and saved drafts remain available. No care content is sent to an external AI service.

Local reminders use generic notification wording. The widget temporarily stores check-in entries in storage shared with the app, then clears them after import.

Sharing, printing and dictation

You can preview a PDF and choose to share, print or save it through iOS. Documents may include identifying health information from your notes and any information you choose to include. Recipients and services you select receive those copies under their own arrangements. The app cannot recall copies you have shared.

Using iOS dictation is governed by your device settings and Apple’s processing. It may involve Apple services. Review the privacy information supplied by iOS before using dictation for sensitive content.

Backups and device changes

You can export an encrypted backup containing records, professional photos and saved letter originals. Unfinished letter reviews are not included. If required files are missing, the app labels the backup partial. Backups use AES-256-GCM encryption with a passphrase-derived key. Keep both the file and passphrase safe: a forgotten passphrase cannot be recovered by Max.

You choose where to save or send the backup. The chosen storage provider may process that file. Exported copies remain until you remove them yourself.

The app requests that iOS exclude care-data files from iCloud and computer device backups. This exclusion and iPhone-to-iPhone transfer behaviour have not been independently verified on a physical device. Some small settings may be included in ordinary device backups. Do not rely on automatic device backup or transfer to preserve your care records; export your own backup before changing phones or deleting the app.

Security

Care records are stored in the app’s private storage using iOS file protection; the app does not add its own encryption to ordinary stored records. Encrypted backup exports are separate. An optional app lock uses iOS authentication, such as Face ID or your device passcode. The app displays a neutral app-switcher view. No security measure guarantees complete protection: protect your device, backup files and passphrase.

Retention and deletion on the device

Records remain until you edit or delete them. Deleted notes remain in Recently deleted for 30 days and are then removed at a subsequent app launch. Other deleted records are removed through their relevant delete controls. Text copied from a letter into a retained history entry remains until you delete that entry too.

The app can retain local migration copies of records to support recovery after a storage-format upgrade. These copies remain until Delete all data. Unfinished letter reviews remain until saved, discarded or cleared with Delete all data.

Delete all data removes local care records, drafts, migration copies, photo and letter files, widget data and reminder schedules. The app-lock switch and language preference remain. File-removal errors may prevent complete removal; contact support if deletion appears incomplete. Temporary export files are normally removed after the share flow; interruption may leave temporary files until cleared by the app or iOS.

Deleting records or uninstalling the app does not remove PDFs, backups or other copies you saved or sent elsewhere. Manage those copies with the recipient or service concerned.

Earlier TestFlight builds with iCloud sync

Some earlier betas, including 0.2.5 and 1.0.0 (13), offer optional private iCloud sync, off by default. When enabled, they sync saved care information through your own private Apple CloudKit database. Drafts and unfinished letter reviews are excluded. These are different builds from the local-only V1 described above.

If you used sync, installing build 14 or uninstalling the app leaves the old iCloud copy in place. Build 14 cannot read or delete that copy. In older sync builds, cloud deletion is attempted only while sync remains enabled, and an offline or failed deletion may not be reported. Do not assume that local deletion also removed cloud data. Contact privacy@roomtosay.org for help identifying the applicable version and options; Max cannot directly access or delete records in your private iCloud database.

Apple distribution and diagnostics

The app has no separate crash-reporting or analytics SDK. Apple may nevertheless process distribution, diagnostic and usage information through the App Store, TestFlight and device services. TestFlight can provide Max with tester information, usage data, crash reports and feedback, including screenshots you submit. Max uses this information to investigate problems and improve the app. Avoid including health records in feedback.

See Apple’s TestFlight privacy information and Apple’s privacy policy for its processing and retention. Apple controls retention of information in its own services.

Children, parents and carers

Room to Say is intended to support people preparing for their own care and parents or carers preparing with someone else. It has no age gate, parental-consent flow or separate child profile. An app age rating does not establish consent to process or share someone else’s information.

Parents and guardians should supervise use where appropriate, especially exports, dictation and contact with support. Enter or share another person’s information only where you are entitled to do so and respect their wishes and privacy. Please do not send children’s health records to the support inbox.

This website

The website has no account, enquiry form, advertising tools or analytics scripts. Its preparation checklist runs in your browser; selections are not saved or transmitted by the website code.

GitHub Pages hosts the site. Cloudflare provides DNS and domain services and redirects roomtosay.app to this site. These providers may process technical request information such as IP addresses for delivery and security. See GitHub’s privacy statement and Cloudflare’s privacy policy. Their processing locations and retention are described in their policies.

Support and privacy enquiries

If you email Max, your address, message and any attachments are processed to answer your request. Published addresses forward through Cloudflare Email Routing to Gmail. Max is currently the only person with access to that inbox. These providers may process information outside Poland or the EEA under their applicable service terms and transfer arrangements. See Google’s privacy policy and Cloudflare’s policy linked above.

Max relies on legitimate interests in answering enquiries, maintaining the service and resolving technical problems for ordinary support correspondence and diagnostic review. Applicable legal obligations may also require retention. Please avoid sending sensitive health information; it is not needed for most support questions. If it is received, Max will minimise its use and address deletion or any necessary permission with you.

Resolved email enquiries are retained for 12 months after closure, then deleted, unless a specific ongoing issue, legal obligation or legal claim requires longer retention. Max does not sell enquiry information or use it for advertising.

Your privacy rights

For personal information Max processes, you may request access, correction, deletion or restriction, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Portability and withdrawal of consent apply where the relevant legal conditions are met. Contact privacy@roomtosay.org; Max may need proportionate information to verify a request. You can also complain to your local supervisory authority, including Poland’s Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).

Max cannot retrieve, correct or export local care records from your phone. Use the app’s editing, export and deletion controls for those records. For information held independently by Apple or another provider, contact that provider.

Changes to this policy

This page will be updated when the app’s processing or operator changes. The date and version scope above identify which release this policy describes. Contact Max if your installed version behaves differently from this description.
