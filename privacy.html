PRIVACY & YOUR INFORMATION

Clear about privacy.

Draft website · app policy not yet finalised

This page describes the current website code. The app’s complete privacy policy must be published here before launch, once its data handling and operator details have been confirmed. This draft is not ready for App Store submission.

This website

This version has no accounts, enquiry forms, advertising tools, or analytics scripts. The preparation checklist runs in your browser. Its selections are not saved or transmitted by the website code.

Opening the site still involves requests to the hosting provider. The live hosting provider, its processing, and any domain services will be identified here when hosting is configured.

Designed with your privacy in mind

Privacy is a design priority as we build Room to Say. We plan to process appointment summaries on your device. Storage, backups, syncing, and sharing are still being finalised; we will explain how they work before launch.

The Room to Say app

The app is in development. Its privacy policy needs to explain the information it processes, where that information is stored, who can access it, and the choices available to you. We will publish those details before the app launches.

This website does not ask you to submit health information.

What needs to be confirmed before launch

  • The identity and contact details of the organisation responsible for the app and website.
  • Account information, health notes, appointment details, and any other information the app processes.
  • Storage, backup, sync, exports, and any AI processing, including third-party services where used.
  • Why information is used, who receives it, and where it is processed.
  • Retention, deletion, consent, and the choices and rights available to users.
  • How use by young people or carers is handled.
  • The live website hosting provider and its data practices.

Privacy enquiries

A public privacy contact will be added before launch. Until then, no personal or health information can be submitted through this website.
