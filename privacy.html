PRIVACY & YOUR INFORMATION

Clear about privacy.

Public beta · full app policy still being finalised

This page explains website behaviour and the current beta’s storage, sharing and recovery limits. The complete app privacy policy and operator details are still being finalised; this page is not ready for App Store submission.

This website

This version has no accounts, enquiry forms, advertising tools, or analytics scripts. The preparation checklist runs in your browser. Its selections are not saved or transmitted by the website code.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Cloudflare provides domain registration and DNS, and handles redirects from roomtosay.app. Visiting the website or a redirect involves requests to the relevant providers, which may process technical information such as your IP address and request details. See GitHub’s privacy statement and Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

The current public beta

The beta stores your notes and care information locally on your iPhone and works offline without a Room to Say account. AI summary generation and iCloud sync are disabled in distributed beta builds. There is no automatic cross-device recovery through Room to Say sync.

Your words and sharing

You can prepare a brief manually or create a PDF of selected notes without AI. Preview a document before using the system share sheet. The app does not automatically send your notes to your clinician. Notes can contain identifying details, so an exported document is not necessarily anonymous.

Sharing a document, saving a backup to a service you choose, emailing us or submitting TestFlight feedback involves the receiving service. Those choices are separate from local note storage. Avoid including personal health information in bug reports and screenshots.

Back up before changing phones

Profile → Back up and restore lets you export and import an encrypted backup. Keep the file and its passphrase safe; both are needed to restore. Export a backup before deleting the app, changing phones or upgrading. Do not rely on an unverified future sync feature to recover your information.

The uploaded 0.2.2 update includes saved letters and original files in encrypted backups, but not unfinished letter imports. A 0.2.2 backup cannot be restored into 0.2.1. Check TestFlight for the version available to you.

Optional app lock

An optional Face ID or Touch ID app lock with device-passcode fallback is available in Profile → Privacy. The feeling widget can save a check-in separately from opening the app. Real-device widget and app-lock checks remain part of beta testing; these controls are not a guarantee of complete privacy.

Still in development: private iCloud sync and AI

Optional private iCloud continuity is the focus of the planned 0.2.3 work. Real CloudKit service behaviour, original letter-file sync and decryption on a second device still require integration and verification. Sync remains disabled in the public beta. Sync is not an independent backup; no guaranteed recovery or end-to-end encryption claim is made here.

AI summary generation is also disabled while it is being reworked. The experimental direction is on-device processing, with no cloud AI. Manual briefs and notes documents remain available without AI.

About this privacy information

This page has been updated against the app-build record on 29 October 2026. It describes current beta limits and website behaviour; it is not a completed app privacy policy. Operator details, retention and deletion information, and the remaining disclosures below still need to be finalised. This website does not ask you to submit health information.

Privacy enquiries

For privacy and data questions, email privacy@roomtosay.org. Please avoid including sensitive health information in your first message.

Emails sent to our published addresses are forwarded through Cloudflare Email Routing to a Gmail inbox. The information you choose to send is processed by those email services, separately from app notes. The website itself has no submission form.
